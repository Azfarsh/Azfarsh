## 👋 About Me

I'm an **M.Sc. Data Science student at Hochschule Bielefeld (HSBI), Germany** and a **B.E. Information Technology graduate** (Sinhgad College of Engineering, Pune, CGPA 8.69/10). I build production-grade, **LLM-based agentic systems**, data pipelines and full-stack AI products.

- 🤖 Former **AI & ML Intern** at EkamVistar Consultancy LLP, building multi-agent pipelines with LangGraph + Gemini
- 🚀 Founder of **Printmax**, an AI-powered SaaS platform adopted by **100+ students in Pune**
- 🧠 Interested in **LLM inference, agent observability and modern AI infrastructure**
- 📄 Co-author of a published research paper on LLM-based data wrangling
- 🌍 Based in **Bielefeld, Germany** · Languages: English (fluent), German (A1, learning)
- 🤝 Open to **working-student roles, internships and collaborations** in AI/ML and data engineering
